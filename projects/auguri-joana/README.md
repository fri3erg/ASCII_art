# auguri-joana 🎂

A birthday card for Joana, made to be opened on a phone. The Portuguese flag unrolls from the hoist and
keeps waving behind everything, with the armillary sphere and the shield where the green meets the red.
"Auguri Joana" writes itself in glowing handwriting, letter by letter, and then a pastel de nata rises into
the middle and turns slowly: flaky golden pastry with its layers on the side, yellow custard with the dark
scorched spots on top, a pinch of cinnamon and a bit of steam.

Underneath sits a plate of pasta with chicken, which she doesn't like, so two red strokes cross it out with a
little shake, and every few seconds the X throbs again just to make the point. Confetti in the flag's colours
falls and small sparkles come and go.

It's laid out for a phone held upright. On a computer you see the same stacked picture in the middle, with
the flag filling the rest of the screen.

## putting it online

No photos here, so a normal Vercel import works: set **Root Directory** to `projects/auguri-joana`,
framework **Other**. Or run `npx vercel --prod` from this folder. Adding `?t=10` to the address jumps 10
seconds in.
